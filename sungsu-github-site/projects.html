Project 01

Heat transfer · MATLAB

Investigating Optimal Heat Distribution: Temperature Gradient Simulation of Heat Packs in Sleeping Bags

This project analyzes the optimal placement of heat packs within a sleeping bag by simulating temperature gradients across different spatial configurations.

A two-dimensional numerical heat transfer model in MATLAB evaluates three heat-pack placement systems, accounting for the thermal properties of the sleeping bag’s inner and outer shells, internal air convection, and the pack’s heat generation.

Project 02

Separation process design

Design of Separation System: Acetone Production from Isopropanol

This project designs a comprehensive separation process to produce high-purity acetone from a reactor mixture containing isopropanol, water, hydrogen, and propylene.

The system uses a sequential multi-step approach to isolate components according to their physical properties.

  1. 01Absorption. Water separates condensable liquids from the gas mixture.
  2. 02Multicomponent distillation. The liquid stream is processed to recover acetone.
  3. 03Pressure-swing distillation. A two-column system resolves the pressure-dependent isopropanol–water azeotrope and recovers unreacted isopropanol for recycling.
  4. 04Membrane separation. A size-selective zeolitic imidazolate framework-8 (ZIF-8) membrane separates hydrogen and propylene.
